# SBBreakers NFL Trend Dictionary — Pro Tracking Version

Condensed version for fast handicapping review. Keeps only the fields most useful for screening and weekly tracking.

## 1. Rematch under -> over bounce
- **Bet type:** Total / Over
- **Trigger:** Teams meeting again within 35 days after the previous matchup went under the total by more than 16 points.
- **Record:** 37-9-1 OVER
- **Sample:** 47 games
- **Confidence:** A
- **Link:** https://x.com/SBBreakers/status/2012552316252037603
- **Quick note:** Strong rematch total angle suggesting the market overcorrects after an extreme under in the first meeting.

## 2. Playoff defense gem -> over
- **Bet type:** Total / Over
- **Trigger:** Teams coming off a playoff win where they allowed fewer than 10 points; stronger subset when they allowed fewer than 7.
- **Record:** 29-8 OVER; subset 17-2 OVER
- **Sample:** 37 games; subset 19 games
- **Confidence:** A
- **Link:** https://x.com/SBBreakers/status/2015450130938274089
- **Quick note:** Excellent playoff total signal; often a buy-low on offensive environment after a dominant defensive showing.
